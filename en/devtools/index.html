Documents

  • CPF generatorGenerate valid random Brazilian CPF numbers, formatted or not, by issuing state. Free, runs in your browser and meant for software testing.
  • CNPJ generatorGenerate valid random Brazilian CNPJ numbers, in the traditional numeric format or the new alphanumeric CNPJ. Free, in your browser, for software testing.
  • CPF/CNPJ validatorPaste a Brazilian CPF or CNPJ (numeric or alphanumeric) and instantly check that its check digits are correct. Free, nothing is sent to a server.

Generators

  • UUID generatorGenerate v4 (random) and v7 (time-ordered, great for primary keys) UUIDs. In bulk, with or without hyphens, right in your browser.
  • Password generatorCreate strong random passwords by choosing length and character types. Generated with the browser's cryptographic API, nothing is sent to a server.

Text & data

  • JSON formatterValidate, beautify and minify JSON, pointing out where the error is. Sorts keys, runs in your browser and never uploads your data.
  • Base64 / URLEncode and decode text as Base64, Base64URL and URL (percent-encoding) with UTF-8 support. Free and in your browser.
  • Regex testerTest JavaScript regular expressions with highlighted matches, capture groups and flags. Live results, right in your browser.

Debugging

  • JWT decoderDecode the header and payload of a JSON Web Token and see issue and expiry dates. All local: the token never leaves your browser.
  • MD5 / SHA hashCompute MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 of any text (UTF-8). Free, instant and nothing is sent to a server.
  • Unix timestampConvert Unix timestamps (seconds or milliseconds) to readable dates and dates back to timestamps, with ISO 8601, UTC and local time.

Generated numbers are fictitious and meant only for testing and development. Do not use them for fraudulent purposes.
